03 / Projects

Things I've built

Featured

Semiconductor Supply-Chain Research Pipeline & Local RAG Search

A resilient, multi-source financial research pipeline — pulling SEC EDGAR XBRL filings, Yahoo Finance, Alpha Vantage, and FMP data — that maintains full financial statements for 72 semiconductor supply-chain companies (~184K financial facts, 37K daily price bars) with retry/backoff, stale-lock detection, and unattended orchestration. On top of it, a hybrid semantic search engine and a local GPU speech-to-text pipeline for podcast sources with no published transcript.

  • Root-caused a ticker-tagging bug where 75.7% of 3,973 tagged mentions were false positives; rebuilt the matcher with cashtag/capitalization-aware evidence tiers, cutting mismatched tags to 1,832 while recovering 11 missed tickers — verified by a 30-test regression suite.
  • Designed a hybrid semantic search engine (local embeddings + Postgres full-text, fused via Reciprocal Rank Fusion) over ~27K chunked documents, raising recall@5 from 0.917 to 1.000 and MRR from 0.889 to 0.958 on a scored benchmark.
  • Deployed local GPU speech-to-text (faster-whisper, int8) at ~75x realtime, cutting a ~2,200-episode transcription backlog to 40–50 compute-hours via resumable runs.
